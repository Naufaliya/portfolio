About The Project

Heavy industrial environments such as steel manufacturing plants carry high operational risks where strict compliance with Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) protocols is critical. This project focuses on building a high-precision computer vision dataset tailored for the GYS Steel facility to automatically track and identify workers who are missing essential safety gear—such as safety helmets, reflective vests, and protective attributes—in real time.

Because industrial CCTV footage involves complex lighting, heavy machinery backgrounds, and varying camera angles, constructing a clean, domain-specific dataset was the foundational step to ensure reliable Object Detection model performance.

Goals

  • Detecting PPE compliance violations automatically (missing helmets, vests, and safety attributes) across active steel plant zones.
  • Reducing reliance on manual CCTV monitoring by enabling automated safety alerts for Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) teams.
  • Building a well-balanced, accurately labeled training dataset capable of handling occlusions, low light, and industrial visual noise.

The User & Audience

The dataset and resulting detection pipeline are built to serve key operational teams within the steel manufacturing plant:

  • HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) Officers: Safety supervisors who require real-time visibility into PPE compliance violations to prevent workplace accidents.
  • AI & Computer Vision Engineers: Model developers who rely on structured, consistent bounding-box ground truth data to train, validate, and fine-tune object detection architectures.

Dataset & Training Scope

  • Video-to-Frame Extraction: Selecting representative frames from raw GYS Steel surveillance footage across different shifts, worker postures, and camera perspectives.
  • Multi-Class Attribute Labeling: Performing precise bounding-box annotations in CVAT (Computer Vision Annotation Tool) to distinguish compliant gear (helmet, safety vest) from non-compliant instances (no-helmet, no-vest).
  • Data Augmentation & Pre-processing: Preparing annotated datasets for augmentation pipelines (brightness, scale, and orientation variations) to improve model generalization on unseen factory footage.

Process

  • Footage Selection & Frame Sampling: Filtered raw industrial video feeds to eliminate redundant frames while capturing edge cases such as partially occluded workers and harsh industrial lighting.
  • Annotation Guidelines & Labeling: Established strict bounding-box tightness and class-naming conventions inside CVAT to maintain labeling consistency across thousands of object instances.
  • Quality Assurance (QA) & Review: Audited annotated frames to fix mislabeled classes, overlapping boxes, or missed small-scale objects in distant camera views.
  • Dataset Export for Model Training: Structured and exported verified annotations into standard object detection formats ready for model training and evaluation.
