About The Project

Urban waste management often suffers from fragmented logistics, lack of transparency in waste sorting, and low public participation due to the absence of direct incentives. Developed by Team Absolute Cinema (Universitas Gadjah Mada), this Smart Waste Management App concept and Business Model Canvas were designed to bridge that gap—earning Semifinalist recognition at Veteran Business Day.

The platform connects everyday households, waste collectors, local waste banks, and recycling industries into a single, verifiable digital ecosystem that turns household waste into measurable environmental and economic value.

Goals

  • Simplifying household waste sorting and pickup scheduling through an intuitive mobile experience.
  • Ensuring transparent, real-time verification of waste types and weights using QR codes and digital scales.
  • Increasing community participation by converting verified waste deposits into tangible rewards and environmental impact metrics.
  • Creating a sustainable multi-stakeholder business model that aligns economic incentives across citizens, waste banks, and recycling partners.

The User & Audience

The ecosystem is designed around two primary user groups with distinct operational needs:

  • Urban Households (Ages 19–65): Environmentally conscious residents in densely populated areas who need a convenient, transparent way to schedule waste pickups and track their personal environmental impact.
  • Waste Management Stakeholders: Field waste collectors, local waste banks (Bank Sampah), recycling factories, and municipal environmental agencies (Dinas Lingkungan Hidup) requiring structured supply routing and verified transaction logs.

System & Business Scope

  • End-to-End Waste Tracking (Track): Users categorize waste (plastic, paper, glass, metal, organic), estimate weight, and pin pickup locations with live status updates.
  • On-Site Digital Verification (Verify): Field collectors scan user-generated QR codes and log digital scale readings to prevent data manipulation and ensure accurate sorting.
  • Smart Supply Routing (Route): Automated distribution logic directs sorted materials to the nearest partnered waste bank or specialized recycling facility.
  • Gamified Incentive Engine (Reward): Verified deposits automatically credit user points that can be redeemed for daily vouchers, e-wallet balances, or partner discounts alongside CO2 reduction stats.
  • Multi-Dashboard Architecture (Connect): Dedicated interfaces tailored for citizens, logistics drivers, and waste bank administrators, supported by B2G contracts, transaction commissions, and data insights.

Process

  • Problem & Stakeholder Analysis: Identified bottlenecks in conventional waste collection—specifically the disconnect between household sorting efforts and downstream recycling facilities.
  • Business Model Architecture: Structured the 9-block Business Model Canvas (BMC) defining value propositions (T-V-R-R-C), key partnerships with local government and recyclers, cost structures (CAPEX/OPEX), and multi-stream monetization.
  • User Flow & UI/UX Prototyping: Designed high-fidelity mobile interfaces covering the complete user journey—from home impact dashboards and map-based pickup scheduling to QR code confirmation receipts.
  • Pitching & Validation: Evaluated against business viability and social impact criteria, advancing to the Semifinal stage at Veteran Business Day.
